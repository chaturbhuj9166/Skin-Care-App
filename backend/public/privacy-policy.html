Privacy Policy

Lock and Key Skin Care · Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SAWARUN TECH PVT LTD ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the Lock and Key Skin Care mobile application (the "App") and related services. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information (patients). Name, phone number, email address (optional), gender, age and profile photo. Patients sign in with their phone number and a one-time code (OTP).

Account information (doctors). Name, email, phone number, specialization, years of experience and profile photo. Doctor accounts are created by our admin team; doctors sign in with email and password.

Consultation ("case") information. Photos and videos of your skin condition that you submit, your answers to our skin-assessment questionnaire, chat messages exchanged with your doctor, the doctor's diagnosis/prescription, and any rating or feedback you leave for a consultation.

Video consultations. When you join a scheduled video call with your doctor, real-time audio and video is transmitted through our video-call provider for the duration of the call. We do not record or store the audio/video of consultations.

Support & account requests. The subject, description and replies of any support ticket you raise, and any account-deletion request you submit.

Device & notification data. A device push-notification token, so we can deliver appointment, chat and status notifications to your device.

Payment records. When you pay the consultation fee, our payment processor Razorpay handles the payment. We receive and keep the order and payment IDs, the amount and the payment status. Your card, UPI or bank details are entered into Razorpay's checkout and never reach our servers.

We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you at sign-in
  • Connect you with an available doctor and manage your consultation case from start to finish
  • Enable chat messaging and video calls between you and your doctor
  • Send you notifications about your case, appointments and messages
  • Respond to support tickets and account-deletion requests
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the App

3. How We Share Your Information

We share case details, photos, videos, questionnaire answers and chat messages with the doctor assigned to your case, so they can provide your consultation. We also use the following service providers to operate the App, each of which processes data only on our behalf:

  • Firebase (Google) — phone OTP verification, push notifications, and secure storage of photos and videos you upload
  • Razorpay — processing of consultation fee payments
  • Agora — real-time transmission of audio/video during consultations
  • Railway — cloud hosting of our servers and database

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or the public.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text, and sessions are protected with signed access tokens. Access to the admin dashboard is restricted to authorized staff.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account and case data for as long as your account is active, so you can access your consultation history. If you request account deletion, we delete your account and associated data as described below.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • Update your profile information at any time from within the App
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time, either in-app (Profile → Delete Account) or online without logging in, at /account-deletion

Once a deletion request is completed, your profile, cases, photos, videos, messages and support tickets are permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older, or who are using the App with the involvement of a parent or guardian for a minor's skin consultation. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children without such involvement.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notified within the App.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us at admin-sawarun@savarun.in.
