Lock and Key Skin Care

A telemedicine platform by SAWARUN TECH PRIVATE LIMITED

Lock and Key Skin Care connects people with certified dermatologists for personalized skin consultations through our mobile app — no clinic visit required. Patients describe their skin concern, share photos or a short video, and are matched with a licensed dermatologist who reviews the case, chats with the patient, and provides a diagnosis and treatment plan. Live video consultations are also available.

We provide online medical consultations only — we do not sell skincare products, cosmetics, or any prescription medications directly through this platform.

Pricing

Each case submission costs ₹399, paid securely through Razorpay at the time of submission. This is a one-time professional consultation fee — not a subscription or platform fee — and it covers a licensed dermatologist's case review, diagnosis, written treatment plan, in-app chat with the doctor, and access to a live video consultation for that case. See our Refund Policy for when this fee is refundable.
