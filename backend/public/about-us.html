About Us

Lock and Key Skin Care

Lock and Key Skin Care is a telemedicine platform built to connect people with certified dermatologists for personalized skin consultations — without needing to visit a clinic.

What We Do

Through the App, patients describe their skin concern, share photos or a short video, and are connected with a licensed dermatologist who reviews the case, chats with the patient, and provides a diagnosis and treatment plan. Patients can also have a live video consultation with their doctor when needed.

Our Mission

To make expert dermatological care simple, private and accessible — so that getting a skin concern looked at by a qualified doctor takes minutes, not a trip to a clinic.

Pricing

Each case submission costs ₹399, paid securely through Razorpay. This is a one-time professional consultation fee that covers a licensed dermatologist's case review, diagnosis, written treatment plan, in-app chat, and access to a live video consultation for that case — see our Refund Policy for details.
